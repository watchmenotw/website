Our approach

A safety culture, not a security operation

Church safety is not about fear, policing or being hyper-secure. It is about an environment where people feel safe, cared for and protected while seeking Jesus, worshiping and being part of a community.

People should notice the peace, not the security.

Ministry-centered

Safety supports ministry and never overshadows it. We remove distractions, risks and threats so worship comes first.

Hospitality and awareness

A strong safety culture blends welcome and watchfulness at every door.

Shared responsibility

Staff and volunteers all carry a sense of care for others: ushers who know CPR, children's volunteers who follow check-in policy, members who report concerning behavior.

Prepared, trained and calm

Trained teams for medical, security and emergency response. Scenario practice without stirring fear. Plans for mental-health crises, and de-escalation with dignity.

Protecting the vulnerable

Children and students, the elderly and disabled, and those in crisis come first in every plan.

Led by biblical values

Security with humility, compassion, discernment and prayer.

Hospitality

  • Greeting people warmly
  • Helping guests feel seen
  • Compassion first

Awareness

  • Watching entrances and behaviors
  • Trained to recognize red flags
  • Preparedness behind the scenes

What we do

Assess, build, train and lead

Every engagement starts with seeing your campus and your people clearly, then builds from there at the pace your church needs.

Assess
  • On-site vulnerability assessment with a written, prioritized report
  • Camera, door-access and technology review
  • Vendor coordination and install oversight
Build
  • Safety team structure, roles and selection criteria
  • Standard operating procedures and emergency plans
  • Policy review aligned with Tennessee law
Train
  • Firearms qualification and scenario training for armed teams
  • Tennessee use-of-force law briefings
  • Medical response, de-escalation and child safety
  • Staff, usher and volunteer awareness
  • Women's situational awareness classes
Lead
  • On-site Sunday observation and team coaching
  • Coaching for pastors and safety-team leaders
  • Integrating the team into the life of the church

Annual programs

Three ways to partner

Each program is a 12-month partnership shaped around your church. Every service is also available on its own. Contact us to talk through what fits your church.

Sentry

For churches starting out or checking their gaps.

  • On-site vulnerability assessment and written report
  • One team or staff training session
  • Two leadership check-ins
  • Phone and email support

Watchman

For churches building or rebuilding a safety team.

  • Assessment and report
  • Team structure, SOPs and emergency plan built with you
  • Quarterly team training
  • Quarterly firearms qualification
  • Quarterly Sunday coaching visits

Guardian

For churches with an active armed safety team.

  • Twelve training sessions a year, including firearms and scenario training
  • Monthly on-site Sunday assessment and coaching
  • Ongoing SOP and emergency plan refinement
  • Priority support
